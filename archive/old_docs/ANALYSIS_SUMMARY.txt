2. TX LOCK MECHANISM ** CRITICAL DISCOVERY **
   Location: Byte 22 (offset +0x16) in each 64-byte record

   Values:
   - 0xFF (255) = TX ENABLED  (transmit allowed)
   - 0x00 (0)   = TX DISABLED (transmit locked)
   - 0x08       = Partial restriction (1 occurrence)
   - 0xBF       = Partial restriction (1 occurrence)

3. CHANNEL RECORD STRUCTURE (64 bytes)

   Offset | Size | Field
   -------|------|----------------------------------------
   0x00   | 4    | RX Frequency (32-bit LE, Hz)
   0x04   | 4    | TX Frequency (32-bit LE, Hz, 0=simplex)
   0x08   | 2    | RX CTCSS/DCS tone
   0x0A   | 2    | TX CTCSS/DCS tone
   0x0C   | 4    | Unknown flags
   0x10   | 1    | Power/step
   0x11   | 1    | Bandwidth
   0x12   | 1    | Modulation
   0x13   | 1    | Scrambler
   0x14   | 1    | Busy lock
   0x15   | 1    | Squelch/signal
   0x16   | 1    | *** TX PERMIT FLAG *** (0xFF=ON, 0x00=OFF)
   0x17   | 1    | Mode/type (0x02=FM, 0x04=USB)
   0x18   | 16   | Channel name (ASCII, null-padded)
   0x28   | 1    | Scan list
   0x29   | 1    | Squelch mode
   0x2A   | 1    | TOT timer
   0x2B   | 1    | TOT rekey
   0x2C   | 1    | Optional signal
   0x2D   | 1    | DTMF ID
   0x2E   | 1    | PTT ID
   0x2F   | 1    | Busy lock out
   0x30   | 16   | Reserved/unknown
